# Research Protocol

## Status

This is the initial protocol. Exact preprocessing choices and primary comparisons will be frozen before benchmark results are generated. Any later change will be versioned and justified.

## Study Identity

**Publication:** J. V. Biju, A. P. Mallya, and P. Kirubanantham, "Prediction of Cardiac Disease Using Quantum Enhanced Ensemble Learning Approach," 2025 International Conference on Innovative Trends in Information Technology (ICITIIT), DOI `10.1109/ICITIIT64777.2025.11041018`.

**Reported result:** 90.16% accuracy on the Cleveland dataset. This is treated as a publication claim, not as a result generated by this repository.

## Dataset

Primary source: UCI Heart Disease dataset, DOI `10.24432/C52P4X`, CC BY 4.0.

The dataset contains four commonly used hospital cohorts: Cleveland, Hungarian, Switzerland, and Long Beach VA. The first benchmark will use the documented Cleveland subset. Cross-cohort analysis will be secondary and will only compare fields with defensible harmonisation.

## Outcome

The UCI `num` target will be transformed to binary presence versus absence of diagnosed heart disease for the primary comparison, with the transformation recorded in every manifest. Ordinal severity analysis may be added as a separate exploratory track and will not be mixed with the binary benchmark.

## Leakage Controls

- split generation precedes learned preprocessing;
- imputation, scaling, dimensionality reduction, feature selection, and calibration are fitted using training data only;
- duplicate and near-duplicate policies are declared before modelling;
- test folds are never used for hyperparameter selection;
- cached quantum kernels are keyed by exact split and transformation fingerprints.

## Primary Comparison

The primary question is whether the bagged QSVC shows a reliable improvement over a tuned classical RBF-SVC under identical outer folds and feature budgets. Logistic regression is the transparent reference baseline.

No superiority claim will be made from a point estimate alone. Differences will be reported with paired uncertainty and compute cost.

## Validation

- repeated stratified cross-validation for Cleveland;
- nested tuning for models that require hyperparameter selection;
- fold-level paired estimates;
- bootstrap confidence intervals over out-of-fold predictions where appropriate;
- separate hospital-held-out experiments as transportability stress tests.

## Interpretability

Explanation methods will match the model and claim. Global permutation importance and stable feature summaries will be primary. SHAP may be used for compatible classical surrogates or models, with any surrogate limitation clearly labelled. Quantum-kernel explanations will not be presented as direct causal or clinical evidence.

## Responsible Interpretation

The project evaluates methods on historical research data. It does not establish clinical effectiveness, causal relationships, safety, or deployment readiness. Sample sizes and subgroup counts will be shown, and estimates below a declared support threshold will be suppressed or marked exploratory.

## Reproducibility Record

Every benchmark artefact will include:

- dataset DOI and file checksum;
- source cohort and row counts;
- target transformation;
- split identifiers and seeds;
- preprocessing and model configuration;
- software and hardware environment;
- runtime and peak-memory estimates;
- output hashes and code revision.
